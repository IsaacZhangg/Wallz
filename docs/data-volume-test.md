# Pre-declared test: is data volume the binding constraint?

Declared 2026-07-25, before any results exist. Every prior plateau hypothesis
(legacy-data dilution, per-round overtraining, weak search) was falsified by
measurement; the surviving hypothesis is that the 30M net at ~65K fresh
positions per round is data-starved. The `isaac-pc` generation node exists to
test exactly this, so the success criteria are fixed in advance.

## Design

**Trigger:** run the suite once the replay directory holds ≥ 1,000,000
positions generated by the kata recipe *after* round 31 (chunk 82 onward),
with training rounds interleaved throughout accumulation (roughly one round
per 2-3 fresh chunks, gateless, 3,000 steps, 60K window). Expected wall time:
about one week of 24/7 PC generation.

**Checkpoint under test:** the then-current `best.pt`, frozen and tagged
before any evaluation game is played.

**Suites (both colors, fresh seeds, Wilson 95% CIs):**

1. **Classical-engine KPI** — `scripts/match_vs_classical.mjs`, classical at
   production adaptive settings (≤1.5s/move), WallZero at 160 sims and at
   800 sims, 20 games each. Baselines to beat: 0.00 at 160 sims (r31),
   0.05 at 800 sims (r31).
2. **Control suite** — 3 seeds × 200 games at 192 sims, leaf_batch 8,
   seeds `1_170_001..3`, vs `best-gate-passed.pt` (r14) and vs the frozen
   round-31 tag. Pooled score with CI, per the standing multi-seed design.

## Pre-committed interpretation

- **Supported:** pooled score vs r31 > 0.55 with CI excluding 0.50, OR any
  nonzero classical KPI improvement ≥ +0.10 over the r31 baselines. Continue
  scaling data; next lever is more generation nodes or bigger windows.
- **Falsified:** flat vs r31 (CI containing 0.50) AND classical KPI within
  +0.05 of baseline after ~15x more fresh data than any prior round enjoyed.
  Data volume was not the constraint; the remaining suspects are network
  capacity/architecture (batch-norm-free migration, nested bottlenecks) and
  qualitatively better targets (uncertainty-weighted playouts), per
  docs/katago-adaptations.md.
- No strength claims from arena promotions or exploratory 40-game runs in
  either case.
